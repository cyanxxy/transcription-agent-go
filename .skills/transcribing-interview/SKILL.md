# Interview Transcription Guidance

Preserve the interview transcript faithfully with speaker turns as spoken. Do not rewrite it into a different structure, and do not relabel speakers unless the names are explicitly provided. Do not turn the audio into a different document type; keep the output transcript-first and faithful to the spoken content.

# Meeting Transcription Guidance

Keep the transcript in meeting form with clear speaker turns. Preserve action items only when they are explicitly spoken; do not infer or invent them. Do not turn the audio into a different document type (such as minutes or a summary); keep the output transcript-first and faithful to the spoken content.
